Frequently Asked Questions

Common Contractor Questions

Are these leads truly 100% exclusive to my business? +
Yes. Absolutely. We do not operate a shared lead marketplace like Angi or HomeAdvisor. Every single Facebook and Instagram ad is launched exclusively under your company's name and branding. Every phone call and estimate request routes directly to your dispatch team.
Do we have to sign a 6 or 12-month lock-in contract? +
No. We believe in earning your business every single month. We start all contractors on the 15-Estimate Growth Pilot. Once you experience the lead quality and close your first system changeouts, we continue on a simple month-to-month agreement.
How fast can our local ad engine go live? +
Our standard deployment protocol takes exactly 72 hours (3 business days). On Day 1 we configure your territory and CRM; on Day 2 we build your custom branded creatives; on Day 3 we launch ads and test dispatch routing.
What happens if another contractor in my city contacts you? +
We enforce a strict one-contractor-per-territory policy. If a competitor reaches out to us from an area you have already claimed, we inform them that the territory is locked and place them on a waiting list.
How does this integrate with our dispatch team? +
We sync directly into your existing workflow. Inquiries can trigger instant SMS alerts to your on-call dispatcher, send structured email notifications, or push directly into ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, or FieldEdge via webhook.
